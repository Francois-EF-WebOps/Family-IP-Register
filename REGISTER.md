## 4. Required fields for each asset

Before changing an entry to **Verified**, record:

- **Identity:** canonical project name, repository, relevant paths, versions, and stable commit/tag references.
- **Creators:** named human authors, inventors, designers, editors, researchers, and other material contributors; role and contribution evidence.
- **Ownership chain:** employer/client/contractor terms, assignments, joint-work arrangements, and any other obligations. Do not infer ownership from account control.
- **Rights and licence:** exact licence, version, date applied, scope, copyright notices, trademarks, and third-party/open-source components.
- **Disclosure:** earliest known public disclosure, URL, commit/release, date, audience, and what technical or creative detail was disclosed.
- **Evidence:** primary records and their locations; keep confidential evidence outside this public repository.
- **Commercial signal:** named user/customer/employer/partner where appropriate, actual use, payment, contract, or a clearly labelled unvalidated hypothesis.
- **Decision:** open publication, copyright/attribution, trade secret, trademark, patent review, defensive publication, or no action—with rationale and review date.
