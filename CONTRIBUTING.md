# Contributing

This repository is an evidence-led index and governance record. Contributions should improve traceability without overstating authorship, ownership, inventorship, legal protection or commercial demand.

## Workflow
1. Open or identify a bounded issue with a specific outcome and acceptance criteria.
2. Search existing asset IDs and records before creating new entries.
3. Work on a branch and submit a pull request; do not push directly to `main`.
4. Preserve original source history and distinguish verified facts, contributor statements, repository metadata, inference and unresolved questions.
5. Add stable IDs, dates, source references, provenance, rights status, restrictions and downstream dependencies.
6. Record decisions, alternatives and rationale. Do not silently rewrite historical records.

## Privacy and disclosure controls
- Never publish family personal records, children's information, identity documents, private correspondence, credentials, access tokens or confidential source material.
- Do not publish enabling technical detail for a potentially patentable invention until disclosure implications have been reviewed.
- A public repository or prior public disclosure may affect later protection options; do not claim that making a repository private reverses disclosure.
- Do not treat repository ownership or account control as proof of sole authorship or ownership.
- Do not label an asset commercially cleared unless the rights chain and intended use have been checked.

## Evidence and rights
Use explicit status labels and cite the underlying evidence. Record third-party material, licences, attribution obligations, consent, AI assistance and human verification. Unknown rights remain unknown; do not infer permission from availability.

## Review
Changes to canonical asset records, ownership assertions, licensing, patent candidates, disclosure history or family-sensitive material require maintainer review. If a claim is disputed, preserve the disagreement and evidence rather than silently choosing a side.

## Contribution and ownership
A contribution record documents what a person contributed; it does not itself transfer copyright, patent rights or other ownership. Any assignment, licence change or joint-ownership arrangement requires explicit agreement and a separate record.

## Definition of done
The change is bounded, evidence-linked, privacy-safe, provenance-complete, rights-labelled, reviewed and reflected in the decision log or relevant register.
